Engagement Models

Three scopes.
Flexible economics.

The scope defines what BVC deploys. The economics define how BVC and the sponsor align around the outcome.

Engagement scope
MODEL A

Full Operating Partner

Revenue, Cost and Product Engines deployed through an embedded operating mandate.

  • Typical ARR: $8M–$25M
  • 12–24 month timeline
  • Full transformation agenda
MODEL B

Targeted Engine

One or two engines applied to a specific operating problem or value-creation priority.

  • Defined mandate
  • 6–12 month timeline
  • Cost or GTM most common
MODEL C

Pre-Exit Optimisation

Metrics, retention, reporting and narrative preparation ahead of a transaction.

  • 18–36 months to exit
  • 18–24 month timeline
  • Diligence and narrative focus
