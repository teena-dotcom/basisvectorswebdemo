Pricing is the fastest
EBITDA lever.

Pricing connects customer value, revenue quality and margin performance more directly than almost any other commercial decision.

Established SaaS companies often inherit pricing from an earlier stage of the product. Packaging expands, customer segments change and delivery costs evolve, while the commercial model remains largely untouched.

Begin with customer value

Effective pricing architecture identifies how different customers receive value and connects packaging to the outcomes, usage or capabilities that matter to them.

Protect retention

Repricing should not be treated as a blanket increase. Cohort behaviour, product adoption, competitive alternatives and customer sensitivity should determine sequencing and migration.

Connect pricing to the operating model

Pricing affects sales incentives, discount governance, customer success, implementation and forecasting. Sustainable improvement requires those systems to change together.
