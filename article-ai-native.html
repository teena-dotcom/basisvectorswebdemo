What makes a SaaS business
AI-native?

Adding an AI feature is not the same as transforming how a software company creates and delivers value.

Many SaaS companies are experimenting with copilots, summaries and generative interfaces. These may improve individual interactions, but they do not necessarily change the economics or defensibility of the underlying business.

Start with workflows

An AI-native transformation begins with the work customers and employees are trying to complete. The opportunity is to reduce manual decisions, connect fragmented information and improve the speed and quality of an outcome.

Data readiness is operational readiness

AI performance depends on accessible, trustworthy and governed data. That makes identity, integration, data quality and process ownership part of the operating transformation, not simply technical prerequisites.

Measure business outcomes

The relevant measures are not model novelty or feature adoption alone. They include time saved, errors reduced, customer outcomes improved, margins strengthened and switching costs deepened.
