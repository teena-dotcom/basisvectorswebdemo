BVC Investment Team · Investment Thesis

Why NRR matters more
than current EBITDA.

In established B2B SaaS, retention can reveal product strength and future value that a current profit-and-loss statement cannot.

EBITDA describes the economics of a business today. Net revenue retention helps explain whether its customers are receiving enough value to stay, expand and deepen their dependence on the product.

Retention is a product signal

Strong NRR is not merely a customer-success metric. It can indicate that the product is embedded in recurring workflows, that customers see measurable value and that expansion opportunities already exist within the installed base.

Why BVC is EBITDA agnostic

A company can have weak current margins and still possess a valuable product foundation. If customer retention is resilient and product-market fit is clear, operating improvements across pricing, delivery, technology and GTM may create a credible path to stronger economics.

NRR is not enough by itself

Retention must be examined alongside concentration, contract structure, cohort performance, implementation quality and the cost required to support each customer. The goal is not to rely on one metric, but to understand what it says about product value.
